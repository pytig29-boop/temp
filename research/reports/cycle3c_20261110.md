## Choice of hypotheses (why these, and why no new family name)
The cycle 3A report recommended pausing the atr_channel / donchian 1h entries. I chose three hypothesis lines that are mechanism-driven and use blocks or block combinations not in the ledger: roc_threshold, volume_z, session_kst at 1h, and volume_z with bollinger.
- Family names were **inherited, not new**. S1 and S2 are long momentum entries, and the claim being tested is a filter interaction on M3 continuation. Session-filtered momentum was already screened in `vol_breakout` (camp-000003), so a new family name for S1/S2 would reset the trial count of the same underlying effect (pitfall P-S2). S3 is M4 and stays in `liq_meanrev`. All four were derived through `lessons_derive_hypothesis`.
- Not chosen: cross-venue Upbit lead at 15m. The Dev evidence in int-000002 (gap tails shrinking from 29 % of minutes in 2021 to 0.4 % in 2025) predicts a G1-recent / G4 failure. RSI bounce at 1h was skipped because it is a repeat: rsi(14,25) at 1h was already NO_SIGNAL in camp-000012.
