## Lessons
- Facts (recorded automatically): fact-000001 to fact-000004 (screens) and fact-000005 to fact-000007 (campaigns). `verify()` returned `[]`, so integrity is OK.
- Interpretations. All are unverified, and each cites evidence and names a falsifying test:
  - int-000001 (fact-000001, medium): the 1m Upbit→Coinone lead is real but smaller than the 4 bp cost and lasts about 1 min. Some of it may be a stale-print artefact on Coinone.
  - int-000002 (fact-000007, medium): large Coinone lags behind Upbit are fading, mostly an early-Dev phenomenon; recent samples are too thin to trade.
  - int-000003 (fact-000005, low): the mean-reversion information is real but concentrated in time, and the TP 3 / SL 5 ATR exits may truncate the 120–240 min drift.
  - int-000004 (fact-000006, medium): the breakout information is economically thin. The taxonomy code is PARAM_SPIKE, but in substance the edge is cost-eaten.
- Derived next hypotheses. These are in the same families, were registered via lessons_derive_hypothesis, and were **not run**:
